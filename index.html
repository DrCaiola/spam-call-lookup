📣 Report this caller

Reports are what feed the database you just searched — every one makes the next lookup smarter and builds the enforcement case. It takes about a minute.

  1. Robocall, spoofed caller ID, or scam? → File with the FCC (choose “Phone” → “Unwanted calls”).
  2. Telemarketer ignoring the Do Not Call list? → Report at donotcall.gov — it asks only for the number, date, and time.
  3. Then block it: iPhone — Phone app → Recents → ⓘ → “Block this Caller”. Android — Phone app → hold the number → “Block/report spam”. Or forward spam texts to 7726 (SPAM) to report to your carrier.

Checking federal complaint records…

Data: FCC Consumer Complaints Data (unwanted-call complaints filed with the FCC) and the NANPA area code registry. Everything runs in your browser — the number you enter is sent only to the FCC's public API.

A number with no complaints isn't guaranteed safe (spammers rotate and spoof caller IDs constantly), and a listed number may be spoofed and not belong to the real spammer. Use your judgment. Not affiliated with the FCC. Source on GitHub.
